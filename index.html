Who We Are

A community where chemistry brings us together.

The American Chemical Society (ACS) Student Chapter at Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST) is a student-led community for people passionate about chemistry and the chemical sciences.

As an ACS International Student Chapter, we aim to connect our campus with the wider global chemistry community. Through scientific events, collaborative learning, research conversations, career-focused activities and outreach, we encourage students to explore chemistry beyond the classroom.

We believe that chemistry can help us understand the world, develop innovative solutions and contribute to a more sustainable future.

Our Purpose

More than a student organization.

We create opportunities for students to learn from one another, engage with the scientific community and prepare for the next stage of their journey.

⚗

Promote Chemistry

Make chemistry engaging and accessible through scientific discussions, demonstrations, learning sessions and outreach activities.

⌕

Encourage Research

Inspire curiosity, research awareness, scientific integrity and communication of research ideas.

↗

Develop Careers

Help students explore academic and industrial pathways while strengthening communication, teamwork and leadership.

Our Activities

Learn beyond the classroom.

Our program areas are designed to connect scientific knowledge with practical skills and real-world impact.

01 · Scientific Development

Seminars & Research Talks

Exchange ideas with academics, researchers and fellow students on emerging scientific topics and research practices.

02 · Practical Learning

Workshops & Skill Building

Explore laboratory safety, scientific writing, research communication, presentation skills and professional development.

03 · Career Exploration

Industry & Career Engagement

Discover how chemistry connects with industry, manufacturing, quality control, research and development.

04 · Public Engagement

Science Outreach

Share the excitement of chemistry with wider communities and encourage scientific curiosity beyond campus.

Professional Development

Bridging academic chemistry with the world of work.

Chemistry opens doors to research, industry, entrepreneurship, education and public service. We want students to see those possibilities and prepare for them.

Through career conversations, industry engagement and practical workshops, chapter activities can help students connect their academic learning with professional opportunities.

PROGRAM CONCEPT

Elevate ACS

Industry Immersion & Professional Workshop — a two-part concept designed to connect academic learning with real-world chemical science careers.

  • Industry exposure and facility learning
  • Professional communication and networking
  • Career pathway exploration
  • Inclusive professional development
